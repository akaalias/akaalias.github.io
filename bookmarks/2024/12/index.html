Show HN: Watch 3 AIs compete in real-time stock trading (trading.snagra.com)

A live dashboard where you can watch GPT-4, Claude 3, and Gemini analyze market data and make daily stock trades with real money. Each AI explains its reasoning, and you can compare their different approaches to the same data.

Link: https://trading.snagra.com?utm_source=hn (no signup required)

What you can try right now: - Watch live trades from GPT-4, Claude 3, and Gemini - Read each AI's full analysis and reasoning - Compare their different interpretations of the same market data - Track their real-time performance and win rates - View historical trades and performance metrics

Built this over the holidays to study how different AI models approach financial decisions. Each morning at 9:30 AM EST, the AIs analyze market data and make real trades with $5 stakes.

Technical Implementation: - Next.js frontend with real-time updates - Node.js/Lambda backend for AI processing - PostgreSQL for trade tracking - Alpaca API for automated trading - Consistent prompts for all models

Data Flow: 1. Daily market analysis (9:30 AM EST) 2. Each AI gets identical inputs: - Financial headlines - Market summaries - Technical indicators - Earnings reports 3. AIs provide: - Stock picks with reasoning - Entry/exit conditions - Risk assessment 4. Automated trade execution

Note: This is an experiment in AI behavior, not investment advice. The goal is to study how different LLMs interpret financial data and make decisions with real consequences.

I'll be around to answer questions about the implementation.

Hey Nassim, just finished Skin in the Game. Really enjoyed it. Spot on to what I’m trying to do with don’t die.

On my data, while some markers are more incrementally tied to age than others, fitness markers in general are great proxies for longevity and improved health. VO2 max for example drops only by 5% per decade of life in athletes, compared to 10% per decade in the general population, on average.
pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3556603/  

This is clearly not genetic fate as it is tied to activity levels and lifestyle choices. 
The point around large individual differences stands, hence why I track baseline and trends over longer periods (maximum HR etc) to use as biological age estimates and longevity markers.  

I measure maximum HR in conjunction with VO2max, heart rate recovery HRR, and resting heart rate RHR. My VO2Max is top 1.5% among 18-year olds, my HRR is top 1% among the general population (top 25% compared to elite athletes), and my resting heart rate is best 1% of elite athletes. My trends for all these markers are favorable and constantly improving as well. 

So I agree that an isolated data point does not mean much, but knowing your trends and assessing the full picture empowers you to reliably measure your performance and assess various interventions and protocols for their real effect on your fitness and health.

Here's a guide for you how you can fine-tune a GPT model:

✅️ Step 1: Prepare Your Training Data

Collect Data: Gather input and output data from the company.

Provide questions and answers in pairs as you want the model to respond:

For example:

Input: "What are your business hours?"

Output: "We are open from 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Friday."

Format Your Data: Save your examples in a JSON Lines file (.jsonl). Each line should have a prompt (input) and a completion (output), like this:

{"prompt": "What is your refund policy?", "completion": "You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase."}

Tip: Use JSON editor tools to transform text data into .jsonl data.

✅️ Step 2: Log into OpenAI

Go to OpenAI’s platform. (Not ChatGPT but Dev dashboard)

✅️ Step 3: Upload Your Training File

Navigate to Fine-Tuning Section in the left sidebar.

Upload Data:

Click the "Upload File" button and select your .jsonl file.

✅️ Step 4: Start a Fine-Tuning Job

Create a Job:

After uploading your file, click Create Fine-Tune.

Select the base model (e.g., gpt-4o, gpt-4o-mini) to start fine-tuning.

Configure Settings:

You can add optional parameters like validation files or adjust the learning rate, but defaults work for most cases.

Start Fine-Tuning:

Click “Start Job.” The process may take some time depending on your dataset size.

You can notice good results from 2M to 5M token dataset. (Also, the quality of dataset matters)

✅️ Step 5: Monitor the Progress

Check Status:

The fine-tuning dashboard will show the progress of your job.

Once completed, you’ll see a new fine-tuned model ID.

Use the new model ID in your API calls to generate responses.

For example, specify your model in the API like this:

{"model": "fine-tuned-model-id", "prompt": "You are a Customer Support Expert"}

Quantum gravity is the field of physics that seeks to unify quantum mechanics, which describes the behavior of particles on the smallest scales, with general relativity, which explains gravity and spacetime on cosmic scales.

Since these theories conflict in extreme conditions (like black holes or the Big Bang), quantum gravity aims to create a single framework to describe nature's fundamental forces.

Notable approaches include string theory, loop quantum gravity, and attempts to understand spacetime as emergent rather than fundamental. Despite progress, a complete theory remains elusive.
