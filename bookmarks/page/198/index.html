  • Healthcare is a $10 trillion "problem": Spending is soaring globally but health outcomes such as life expectancy are plateauing—or even declining, and disease prevalence is rising.
  • There is hope (thanks to AI): The rapid progress in AI is sparking a generational shift in how healthcare operates: from automating back office workflows to augmenting decision-making.

The future startups are building towards:

  • Personalized and full-context: We will no longer have to turn to the dark corners of the internet for self-diagnosis; instead, we will all have access to a personal AI health companion that has access to the full context of our health. This is what Livv Health is building towards.
  • Human-centered care: The days of manual note-taking into clunky systems are a relic of the past: startups like Corti and Nabla are eliminating doctors’ time spent on documentation—giving back “the gift of time” to clinicians.
  • AI-augmented: With OpenEvidence providing easy access to medical literature and startups such as Glass Health supporting clinical decision-making, we are poised for a future where every doctor can make the best decision, grounded in evidence and augmented by real-time AI.
