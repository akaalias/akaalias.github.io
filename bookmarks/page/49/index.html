13: Cursor – code editor for refining your MVP

14: Superwall – test paywalls and pricing remotely

15: RevenueCat – track LTV, churn, and revenue

16: Appsflyer – attribute paid ads (critical post-iOS14)

17: Mixpanel / Amplitude – track every user tap and drop-off

Most of these tools are free until you scale.
