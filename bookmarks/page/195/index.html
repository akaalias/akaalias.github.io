Courses, boilerplates, coaching, products targeting other founders..

Entrepreneurship is not a 'follow this checklist' type of thing, but some of these indie hacker influencers make it look like that.

You can identify these tools/products/content bundles by looking at their founder here on Twitter. Are they continuously promoting success and selling a dream only if you buy that product? Is their success the exact thing they're trying to sell you?

Identifying as (only) an indie hacker, building a product for other founders and only doing your 'marketing' on twitter and product hunt is essentially a limiting belief. You're sticking to a comfortable bubble.

Think bigger, don't be too influenced by this indie hacker / founder bubble and don't fall for the buy-this-product-for-success shit.

Bookmarked from Mike Taylor @hammer_mt

When I tell people I am about to publish an O'Reilly book on prompt engineering and AI, their first question is always "won't it be out of date the moment it's printed?". It's something @jamesaphoenix12 and I had to think about, a lot...

So we took the things that were working for us back in 2020 with GPT-3, and ditched any hacks or tricks that stopped working when GPT-4 came out. What was left was a set of timeless principles that are transferrable across models:

1. Give Direction: Describe the desired style in detail, or reference a relevant persona.
2. Specify Format: Define what rules to follow, and the required structure of the
response.
3. Provide Examples: Insert a diverse set of test cases where the task was done
correctly.
4. Evaluate Quality: Identify errors and rate responses, testing what drives performance.
5. Divide Labor: Split tasks into multiple steps, chained together for complex goals.

The whole book is based on these principles, so even if OpenAI drops GPT-5 the week after the book is published, I'm still confident the content will remain relevant. That said, after a year and a half of writing and editing, I'm really looking forward to getting it out there in the world and see what people think!
