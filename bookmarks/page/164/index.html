A rule I follow:

For any important project, give it two 2 years, not 2 months.

You will always hit the same problems:

  • A lack of initial results
  • A rubbish first version
  • The valley of despair
  • The crippling self-doubt
  • The million shiny objections

But the difference between impossible and probable is the timeframe you give yourself.

You can solve almost any issue. You can learn almost any skill. You can achieve almost any goal. But only if you remove the pressure to perform immediately. You gotta give yourself a chance to get going.

Zoom out.

Play long-term games.

Hope this helps,
