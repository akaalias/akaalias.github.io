Bookmarked from Pat Walls @thepatwalls

You can make millions creating content without showing your face.

Bullshit. Bullshit. Bullshit.

Yes, there are exceptions.

But if you decide to go the faceless route, you will be starting with a MASSIVE disadvantage.

Your content, your stuff, everything will need to be 10x better than everyone else, and you will need to get even more lucky, too.

Why? The human brain is wired to recognize faces. Remove that, and you strip away the most important part of your brand.

Faceless youtube channels, faceless Twitter accounts, etc - I do worry about people wasting time on these ideas.

Even if you can get something to work, your stuff is easily copyable, specifically... because it's faceless!

And even if it can get views, it's even harder to monetize.

The reality is 95% of people are scared to show their face online - which is rooted in a fear of failure, a fear of what people will think about them etc.

MASSIVE leverage to get over that fear, show your face, be yourself, etc.

Yes, there are exceptions. The successful faceless people? Their stuff is just that good.
