How I use DeepSeek to analyze my LinkedIn profile.

Step 1: Extract your Linkedin data.

☑ Click on your profile.
☑ Click on "Post impressions".
☑ On the top right, click on "Export".

Step 2: Clean your data.

☑ Open a new sheet.
☑ Upload the extracted data.
☑ Delete the tabs "Discovery" & "Demographics".

Step 3: Go to DeepSeek.

☑ Go to chat . deepseek . com
☑ Upload the new cleaned Excel file.
☑ Toggle on "DeepThink R1", but not "Search".

Step 4: Prompt DeepSeek as a data analyst.

☑ Copy & paste this following prompt:

"You are a data analyst & scientist.

Here are my Linkedin post analytics.

I want to make better decisions. Think in a step by step process on how you will help me out AFTER you analyzed the entire excel file I shared."

☑ Ask as many follow-up prompts as you want.

Here's mine on the video:

"Now that you've shared this, go much deeper in your analysis. Only use the data. Do not use your intuition. You are a data analyst."

Step 5 (Bonus): Rewrite your best posts.

☑ Ask DeepSeek what's your "best posts".
☑ Copy the entire caption from each of them.
☑ Go to EasyGen (app . easygen . io).
☑ Paste one of the caption on "Custom Topic".
☑ Hit "Generate a post" until you love it.

____

The fascinating part? This "data analyst" is free.

And it's the worst it will ever be.
