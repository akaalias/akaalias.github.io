Bookmarked from Kieran Drew @ItsKieranDrew

An idea I’m obsessed about:

Action occurs when pain exceeds fear.

It’s such a powerful concept.

If fear>pain, you don’t do anything (except worry endlessly).

But if pain>fear, you start solving the problem.

Most people wait for the pain to become so great you can no longer tolerate it. Or they increase the pain with self-destructive behaviour until they have no choice but to act.

But a much faster, easier way to win is to reduce the fear instead.

How?

Understand it. Because 99% of fear is not real. It’s a construct of your past experiences and biases.

Reduce it. If you’re scared to take the big step, take the smallest version. Action relieves anxiety.

Hope this helps,
@itskierandrew
