Bookmarked from Nick St. Pierre @nickfloats

Ya, it’s crazy how the whole creative process has flipped

The idea "just because you can, doesn’t mean you will" has been stuck in my head all day

AI lets one person be all these different roles at once, which seems like it should lead to a wave of high quality work

But just scroll through your feed...the gap between what people can do and what they actually create is huge.

It's like anyone can pick up a paintbrush, but that doesn’t mean they’ll paint a masterpiece. Tools don't replace taste, vision, or understanding how things fit together.

Also, the idea that now, instead of being a specialist, you’re expected to be a one person creative team is a lot

Just cause you can do it all doesn’t mean you’ll do it all well. It’s a different skill set, learning how to integrate all these pieces together. Visuals, sound, narrative, etc

I feel like the whole AI explosion has yet to raise the bar for the average quality of creative work, it’s just given more people access to make stuff

And the fact that you can generate stuff with them doesn't make you a creative person or an "artist"

That still has to be earned
