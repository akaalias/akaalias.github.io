When needed I'm always checking word pronunciation in Google Translate, so I've mainly a personal need for a minimal, and simple app that helps check accent/articulation in one click.

🔎Market Research:
Starting from a personal need, I've done a quick market research using @TryAstroApp and AppFollow then I decided to build an app MVP that pronounces words and helps in checking articulations in multiple languages accents.

  • I used my swiftship boilerplate to get analytics, iAP monetization, notifications, and other common features in the app ready-made.

🎨Design/UI:

  • I found a good dribbble design that fits my simplicity/minimalism need, then created a view based on it and edited it to fit the requirements of the app.

✅Metadata & Distribution:

  • I got the app keywords and ASO stuff from Astro and AppFollow.
  • Created the screenshots with AppScreens
  • Generated the app description with chatGPT

... and Voila the app is currently live in the appstore.
💰 After less than 48 hours of distribution, the app already got 3 sales.
