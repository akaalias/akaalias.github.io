Bookmarked from Bindu Reddy @bindureddy

Quiet-STaR Gives AI An Inner Monologue

Quier-STaR teaches language models to think before they speak 🤣🤣

It's like giving an AI an inner monologue, and this approach improves common-sense reasoning but doubles math performance!!

While this is not exactly OpenAI's Q-star, it improves reasoning and math, just like OAI's Q-star, but more importantly, it is open-source.

Pretty sure, OpenAI's Q-star and GPT-5 use techniques like this one to improve reasoning in LLMs

Humans usually pause to think before writing or speaking. Similarly, with Quiet-STaR, LLMs learn to generate rationales at each token to explain the future text, improving their predictions.

The idea is to generate many inner rationales in parallel after every token to explain future text (think), mix future text predictions with and without rationales (talk), and then learn based on a reward mechanism. The reward mechanism teaches the LLM to learn the right rationales.

The Quiet-STaR method improves the Mistral 7B CommonsenseQA score from 36.3% to 47.2% (significant) and doubles its Gsm8K's (math) performance!!

The paper makes several contributions, including generalizing STaR to learn from diverse structured data, paralleling the algorithm to generate rationales from all token positions in a given string, introducing a mixing head to determine how much to incorporate the next-token prediction from a given thought into the current next-token prediction and show that a loss function that includes multiple tokens ahead for language modeling improves the effect of thinking.

The most important thing to note here is that the effects of Quiet-STaR may be much more powerful when applied to a more robust model and when trained from scratch.

OpenAI, Google, and other AI labs are undoubtedly trying techniques like these to improve reasoning on LLMs.

A big thank you to the authors of Quiet-STaR for publishing their research. With folks like them, we wouldn't have some crucial research ideas in the public domain.
