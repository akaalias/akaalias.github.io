Bookmarked from Physics In History @PhysInHistory

Quantum gravity is the field of physics that seeks to unify quantum mechanics, which describes the behavior of particles on the smallest scales, with general relativity, which explains gravity and spacetime on cosmic scales.

Since these theories conflict in extreme conditions (like black holes or the Big Bang), quantum gravity aims to create a single framework to describe nature's fundamental forces.

Notable approaches include string theory, loop quantum gravity, and attempts to understand spacetime as emergent rather than fundamental. Despite progress, a complete theory remains elusive.
