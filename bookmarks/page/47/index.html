Bookmarked from Riley Brown @rileybrown

I'm no longer using claude, chatgpt, or perplexity.

I'm only using claude code.

For literally everything.

And I keep everything organized locally.

The Claude Code + @obsdmd combo is actually bonkers for content creation / research. (Shout out @mckaywrigley, his latest video on youtube is great)

If this is something you want to do, I highly recommend creating a "HQ Examples" file on your computer.

This should be a folder of 30-50 high quality examples of your work, or anyones work that you want to create like, and giving Claude Access.

Giving good examples is worth as much if not more than a really good prompt.

---

This also gives FLEXIBILITY.

If you store everything locally you can port your data into other tools much easier than using chatgpt, claude, and perplexity.

Don't create a claude project, chatgpt project, or perplexity spaces, when you can just create a local folder with a "ReadME" file with a folder of good examples.

You can also create commands. Which i'll talk more about in my next video.

Bookmarked from Dr. Rhonda Patrick @foundmyfitness

Psilocybin shows potential as an anti-aging molecule, with lifespan-enhancing results in human cells and mice observed in a new study.

In vitro, psilocin (psilocybin’s metabolite) extended cellular lifespan by 29% at baseline and 57% at higher doses. It also reduced markers of aging like β-gal activity, boosted sirtuin (SIRT1) levels, lowered DNA damage and oxidative stress, and preserved telomeres.

In vivo, 19-month-old female mice (equivalent to ~60–65 human years) who received monthly psilocybin doses for 10 months had 80% survival vs. 50% in controls. Treated mice also appeared younger—with improved fur quality, more hair growth, and less graying.

Although psilocybin is listed as a Schedule I substance, these findings position it as a novel therapy for healthy aging and open a path toward 'psychedelic-assisted senotherapeutics', interventions that may enhance psychological well-being while also slowing genetic aging.

No matter your stance on psychedelics, these findings should intrigue you.
