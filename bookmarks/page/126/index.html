ADHD isn't a disorder, its a neurological type.

The same wiring that makes you distractible and hyperactive in todays world made you an elite hunter-gather

  • Hypervigilant: Constantly scanning the environment for threats
  • Impulsive: Quick decision-making for hunting & survival
  • Hyperactivity: Necessary for long hunts and physical demands of nomadic life.
  • Hyperfocus: Ability to concentrate intensely on immediate, interesting tasks (like hunting)

These "ADHD" genes were positively selected for until farming & peasantry became the societal norm.

"ADHD" went from being an elite advantage to a maladaptive trait. Hunter's mind, farmers world.

Modern life is a nightmare for somebody hardwired to hunt & gather:

  • Sitting in a classroom for 14 years straight
  • Sitting behind a computer doing repetitive tasks
  • Constantly bombarded with artificial stimuli
  • Overwhelmed by Information overload

The monkey that thrives in the circus has the opposite traits of the monkey that thrives in the wild. Its like putting a shark in an aquarium and wondering why it doesn't survive.

If you label yourself as disordered you’ll never ascend that label. Understand how your brain works and create a life that compliments those unique traits.

Some of the most successful & extravagant people in the world have ADHD and thats not by accident

The solution isn't amphetamines. Its reinventing your life to play games you're uniquely suited to play
