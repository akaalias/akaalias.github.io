BookmarkSaturday, June 1, 2024 · 00:07
Bookmarked from John Rush @johnrush

You can basically apply my startup method:
(I used it for most of my 24 projects)

1. Build b2b tool for yourself & be your own customer.

2. Share publicly how you use it with great details.

3. Produce educational content around your topic with your own examples. People will start following you and ask for advice.

4. Offer early-stage founders a bundle: You + Your Tool. (I gave my users a free SEO audit and marketing help in the early days of ListingBott, SeoBot, and Index Rusher).

5. Repeat 2-4 until you get to 10-50 clients.

6. Now that you have enough happy early adopters, they will spread the word, and you’ll have organic growth.

7. There are two potential failure points here:

  • You can’t really satisfy yourself when using your own product. Iterate until satisfied.
  • you don’t know enough about the topic to give valuable advice to your early adopters. So, learn and practice more.

P.S. I didn't mention SEO, Directories, and other marketing channels because I've covered them in my other posts. If interested, browse my "highlights".

It was foreseeable and only a matter of time. Soon we will be able to prompt our own series and films, and even computer games. Something like: "Create me a horror movie that has a protagonist who suffers from depression and is in love with the daughter of a rich family" - and the AI will create it. The same with computer games.
Movies and games are becoming something very individual. It is exciting to see how the costs will be. If it no longer requires actors, if it no longer requires video game programmers, but only an AI that has costs for generating the games and films, how cheap or expensive will it be?
Culturally, too, completely new questions arise: if we all watch different things, will there still be cinemas? Will "watching movies" even have the character of a social element, or will we become culturally isolated?

All in all, completely new ethical questions are arising. And to be honest, we should address these questions sooner rather than later. The time to debate them socially is now.
