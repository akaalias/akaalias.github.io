My telomeres say I'm 10 years old.
They are regenerated at the vibrancy of a 12 year old.

Data:
Telomeres: 10.27 kilobases
Telomerase activity: 7.7%

Telomeres are like shoelace tips on your DNA, protecting your chromosomes every time your cells divide.

Telomerase is the enzyme that maintains and restores telomere length with each cell division.

Short telomeres = old, damaged cells.
Long telomeres = youth, repair, longevity.

Why it matters:
+ Lower biological age
+ Slower cellular aging
+ Preserved genomic integrity
+ Resistance to entropy

Data sources:
My telomere biological age is 10 years, based on a database of 15,000 other participants who took the test.
