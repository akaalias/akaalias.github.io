Here’s how I recently did ASO for screencut.app

First, I went to aso.dev and checked which keywords my competitors are ranking for (in the “Spy” section). By the way, aso_dev currently has a promotion - you can test the tool for just $1.

I made a few manual tweaks and submitted the update to the App Store. I repeated this process for French, Japanese, Korean, Italian, Turkish, and Chinese
