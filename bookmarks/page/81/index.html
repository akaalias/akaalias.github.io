BookmarkMonday, February 10, 2025 · 15:49
Bookmarked from esther @esterezw

I used to think my life was boring.

  • I have no crazy stories.
  • I haven’t scaled to $10,000/day.
  • I’m not jumping out of a airplane daily.

So what do I talk about?

As I started writing on X….

I’d spend hours searching for the next life changing idea to talk about.

I saw big creators going viral talking about their romance stories and daily life.

It looked so easy for them.

Yet there I was starting at my blank PC screen and feeling like punching it every time I struggled to find interesting ideas to talk about.

So I gave up.

I quit chasing after the next big idea.

And thought I’d give a try to sharing my “boring” life:

  • my work life.
  • a cute pet I saw.
  • story of my classmates.

Somehow, I saw success with it.

My bunny picture went viral.

My story about quitting university got 100’s of people reaching out and sharing relatable stories to mine.

People were reading my “boring” stories.

But why?

This is nothing special….or ground shattering…why would anyone read it?

People want relatability.

They’re more likely to read to your story about getting fired than a story of scaling to $10,000/month because it’s more relatable.

Sure, big goals are cool to share.

But you won’t be jumping out of an airplane every single day.

You don’t need to chase after the next goal to have something to talk about.

Because your life is already the story you’re searching for.

And that small idea you have...

Could potentially change someone’s day.

Share it.
