Bookmarked from Audrey Lo @audrlo

I've built my audience to 15,000 followers.

No paid ads. No paid RTs.

My secret?

Building a community.

Here's how you build an engaged audience from scratch:

Before we get in, this is not a growth post.

I just want you to attract a quality audience that you can actually monetize (the size doesn't matter.)

1/ Focus on the people

You can write the best post in the world.
You can have the best offer in the world.

But nobody will like it if you haven't built those relationships.

Get out there.

Connect with your ideal prospects.

Become friends with everybody else in your niche.

You need a strong foundation.

2/ Establish a big idea

People are attracted to ideas.

Find a unique idea that you can talk about forever.

It doesn't need to be super special.

Just something that's slightly different.

Eventually, your idea becomes your brand.

And whenever someone hears about your idea, they'll think about your brand.

3/ Fight a brand enemy

People get all hyped up when there's someone to fight.

I'm not saying to go spread hate.

Instead?

Find a topic or idea that you disagree with.

Position your solution as the hero.

If your content is bland and boring - nobody will engage with you. Nobody will buy from you.

Add a little spice.

Polarize.

Final note:

Remember, it's not about the audience size.

It's about audience effectiveness.

I prefer a smaller audience with a bigger business.

Versus a bigger audience with a smaller business.

Focus on what matters.

Build the right way. Build the authentic way.

2. RT this post ❤️

Talk soon,
Audrey
