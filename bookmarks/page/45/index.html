Bookmarked from Rohan Paul @rohanpaul_ai

Social media mood barely predicts stock moves, while the plain count of Reddit comments or Google searches explains more of the daily swings.

They found, Sentiment metrics barely matter. Plain counts of Reddit comments and Google searches track price jumps.

Simple crowd metrics, like Reddit comment volume and Google search interest, correlate strongly with and even causally push stock price changes, and the price swings feed that activity in return.

🚀 Why this study

Retail traders on r/wallstreetbets pushed GameStop up by about 7900% in early 2021, so the authors asked whether chatter on that forum can regularly signal price shifts.

They picked GameStop (GME) and AMC, scraped roughly 7 million posts from 2021‑01‑04 to 2021‑03‑31, and kept an eye on comment counts, emoji jokes, and search traffic.

The goal was to see if any of that noise actually leads the tape instead of simply following it.

📊 Building the dataset

Every post mentioning GME or AMC went through cleaning and tokenisation so models could read it.

About 561 563 posts used at least one emoji, with the rocket and diamond‑hands icons topping the chart, because users love shorthand like “to the moon.”

Google Trends supplied a daily interest score from 0‑100 for each ticker, giving a second crowd signal that is not tied to Reddit.
