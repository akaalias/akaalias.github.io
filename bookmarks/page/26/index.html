Here’s the story of how we got here 👇

In the fall of 2022, as we graduated @ycombinator, we were pitching our “stablecoin business banking” idea to skeptical investors at Demo Day. The crypto market was in shambles (FTX collapsed a few weeks later), and anything “blockchain” suddenly looked very risky.

In the following months, @jeapostrophe, @vivek_kumar_15, @akanshj31, and I pivoted our focus to building the most reliable infrastructure crypto-friendly institutions could depend on during volatile times, while we waited for the market’s trust to recover. We had a rare team that simultaneously specialized in cybersecurity, payments and blockchains — domains that would become the backbone of modern onchain finance.

Fast forward to December of last year, everything changed. Almost overnight, crypto went from “too risky” to a policy priority for the government and a strategic priority for institutions and fintechs.

But one asset class attracted disproportionate institutional interest: stablecoins.

Our explosive growth this past year, coupled with the hundreds of conversations we’ve had with payments and finance leaders, taught us one thing: stablecoins are becoming core financial infrastructure faster than people think.

When the GENIUS Act was introduced in Congress earlier this year, we knew it would mark the beginning of a new age in payments and cross-border finance. In the months ahead, safe and convenient digital dollar access and near-instant settlements became the baseline expectation.

But infrastructure providers failed to catch up. Incumbent systems remained optimized for crypto trading and investing use cases, whereas enabling teams to build real-time, onchain payment systems needed fundamentally different design choices and trade-offs.

Real-time onchain finance needed tailored tooling.

PrimeVault is just that: the most reliable, stable and performant stack on the market to build any stablecoin-powered product or treasury workflow.

Stablecoins are rewiring global finance. The change is more insidious (happening inside the finance back-office), which makes it easy for people to miss.

If you’re a bank, fintech, money services business, or even a corporation looking to transform your treasury function from a cost to a revenue center, reach out to us at PrimeVault.

The future shouldn’t wait on infrastructure.
