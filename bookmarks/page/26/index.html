Bookmarked from Jake Mor @jakemor

Hot take from @filippkowalski - should you remove your paywall from onboarding? The answer might surprise you 👇

Disclaimer: Since we don't really know where the paywall is on a per-app, we use d1 paywall rate as a proxy.

Here's the data:

  • n > 1000 (over 1000 apps)
  • 30d net arpu (net revenue per user after refunds, for users who installed 45 days ago to 15 days ago, to clear typical trial lengths)
  • at least 385 paywalled users
  • only looking at Superwall generated revenue
  • removed 5% extremes on both ends (top 50 ARPU apps and bottom 50 ARPU apps removed)

Scatter plot - each data point
Histogram on 10% bins

So there is some truth to what Filip is saying - too high a paywall rate may turn off customers before they get to experience your app.

On the flip side, if less than 50% of people are seeing your paywall on day 1, there is almost no shot you'll have a high ARPU.

So while we can't really answer if onboarding is the right place for it, there is definitely such thing as being "too aggressive" with the paywall.

Personally, I love the way @photoroom_app does it - paywall in onboarding, but only after value is demonstrated.

Photoroom guides the user through removing a background inside onboarding itself, essentially baking in a stripped down version of their product to get users to an Aha! moment prior to showing pricing.

...this is the way!
