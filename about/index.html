Alexis Rondeau

About

I’m Alexis Rondeau, a product engineer and an experimenter at heart. I live and work between Berlin, New York and Marseille, and I build software, teach other people to build it, and run experiments to find out what actually works.

What I do now

I’m Principal Product Engineer at FELS Family Office GmbH.

On the side I run what I call discovery loops: I give an AI agent a hard question, a way to measure the answer and a budget, and let it run experiments until it finds something I couldn’t have written by hand. Recent ones:

  • Can a UAV learn a city by heart? A 3.1 MB neural memory that places a drone over Berlin to within 27 m, with no GPS and no map on board.
  • Evolving a quadcopter frame with a genetic algorithm and Claude as an occasional co-designer, for a frame that flies 16% more efficiently.
  • Teaching a phone-sized language model to ask questions nearly as well as a frontier model, in 96 experiments.
  • Letting an agent discover scheduling rules instead of writing them myself.
