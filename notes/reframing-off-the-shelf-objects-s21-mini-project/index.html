Reframing Off-the-Shelf Objects (S21 Mini-Project)

To build on what’s there already and make it better.

Idea is to buy regular clothes, like from Uniqlo, but customize them uniquely and with a high degree of sophistication (think embedded gold rather than silk-screened) so they become something better and new. Add surprising details.

Improvements

  • What would be better?

    • A different color
    • Small details
    • More colors

  • What’s missing?

    • Having a pocket here

Customized My Everyday Items

Reframe a shoulder-bag as a hip-bag

  • I’ve been looking for a non-douchy hip-bag but couldn’t find anything in Berlin that didn’t make me feel weird wearing
  • I went to Muji and found their shoulder bag for men
  • First I was put off because I didn’t want a shoulder bag but the item itself intrigued me

![IMG20210807150350 1.jpg](/notes/media/IMG20210807150350 1.jpg)

  • I realized I could maybe use the strap to go around my waist and have a DIY hip-bag instead

  • Tested it and it worked very nicely!
