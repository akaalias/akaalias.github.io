HumanCharger Unboxing: First Impressions and Use

My HumanCharger device is finally here, after a long, long, long, long wait.

I’m very excited and happy!

Here are a few things I noticed:

Unboxing and First Impressions:

  • Nice, clean packaging
  • Easy to open
  • Was able to turn it on right away
  • The device itself is made of smooth metal and feels nice
  • The earplugs seem standard, made of plastic and rubber
  • The LEDs are smaller (2mm diameter) than I expected and as bright as I can imagine they get (not blindingly bright though)
  • There are bright LEDs in the ear-plugs and not “fiber-optic cable endpoints” as Oleksii and I had theorized for heat-management.

  • Technical specifications from the booklet:

    • Typical session duration: 12 minutes
    • Average light intensity: > 10,000 lux (within 1cm of the LED)
    • Peak wavelength: 450nm

First Use

  • The earplugs fit comfortably in my ears though I’m not sure if they’re positioned correctly. No guidance found in the booklet.
  • The earplugs got warm but not uncomfortably so.
  • The battery was empty less than 10 minutes after start. I think that it was low on power to begin with, so I’m charging it now. That makes sense, I didn’t expect it to be 100% charged.

So, right now I’m waiting for a re-charge and the experiment will begin by tomorrow morning. I’ll probably write a few more posts on the progress I’m making with the HumanCharger.
