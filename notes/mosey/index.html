Mosey: Walks that start themselves — the world's longest-lived people don't exercise. They simply get up and move every 20 minutes.

The longest-lived people don’t exercise — they get up and move every twenty minutes. I built Mosey to put that nudge into a Mac workday: after twenty minutes at the desk, your walking pad starts on its own, three to twenty minutes, a different walk each time at a pace it sets. Step on when you’re ready, step off whenever you like. Nothing counts down at you, no streaks, no account — and it’s free. Coming to the Mac App Store.
