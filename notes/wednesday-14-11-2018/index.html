Wednesday – 14.11.2018

Wednesday afternoon, at home, not crazy about being here but I think I’m dragging out the cold I got over a week ago, so I took a nap just now and feel better. Didn’t feel up to bar at all this morning and still went on a nice bike-ride with my dad. We visited Factory which I decided isn’t a place that I want to go to anymore. 

I also realized, based on my father’s observation, that the vibe of people sitting alongside long tables in silence at their computers totally feels like a factory. That they look like factory workers, quietly typing along and not talking to each other. 

I guess that’s one of the reasons I’ve felt so alienated there for so long. I just couldn’t put my finger on it. 

So, I think it’ll be best to cancel my subscription this week and avoid paying another $130 per month for a place that I don’t enjoy. I’ll be able to put that money towards things that give me joy and move me forward. It’s kinda sad because the Factory building is really nice. But I also realized that the endless amount of heavy doors you have to open and close to get from A to B really disrupts my flow of thinking and working. 

I also think I will cancel my Drip.com subscription and the Javelin.com subscription, too. In both cases I can use other, cheaper services. I will keep my Clickfunnels subscription because that’s been really working well. It’s a good tool and I’m learning a lot with it. For Drip I can use Mailchimp for which I still have an account. For Javelin I’m already using Evernote instead.

What else…?

Recovering from a cold took me much longer than I expected.

In fact, I’m on day 10, yet still don’t feel 100%. It started two weeks ago. 

I had felt a cold coming up first on Wednesday. 

I felt weak and I noticed how I couldn’t concentrate. 

The next day I felt okay again and forgot about it. (I was still weaker than usual but I didn’t mind.) 

Thursday night I had a sore throat though and I realized I was about to crash. 

Then, Friday morning I felt okay again. I still worked through a bunch of action items, but then at night I finally crashed into bed. 

On Saturday, I woke up sick, with a running nose, coughing. I didn’t leave bed for most of the day and I cancelled plans. 

On Sunday, felt a little better, well enough to see my friend. 

Monday I was back in bed, frustrated, Tuesday I got restless again. 

Wednesday I thought I was fine again but I wasn’t.  

Thursday, I got a lot of work don but didn’t feeling great. 

Friday, Saturday and Sunday felt normal but I still coughed and sneezed. 

Monday, Tuesday still felt weak. 

Which brings us today where I’m back home. 

I wish I was more productive, but I’m also glad to be here. 

I’ll likely take another nap before going for dinner with my dad. 

I thought I had rested enough about a week ago and was on the mend. But it’s taking much longer to get back to normal. 

I should have taken care of myself earlier and much longer.
