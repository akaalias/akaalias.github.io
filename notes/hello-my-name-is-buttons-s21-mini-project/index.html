⭐️ "Hello my name is" Buttons (S21 Mini-Project)

Basically, have a reusable version of the classic “Hello, my name is” sticker as a button with my name on it and wear it while I’m out and about to show that I’m open to conversation by offering my real name.

‘Hello’ Button MVP

Learnings

  • Looks really good with the name writting in Sharpie
  • Felt good, exciting to wear it out
  • Felt vulnerable, authentic and powerful to put my name out into the world

Der normale Sticker

MVP: Stickermule Buttons

  • Größe: 7cm x 4.4cm^1
  • Preis: 10 für $37
  • 10 Bestellt: 📎 Hello.pdf

Question

  • Mit welchem Stift kann ich drauf schreiben?

Weitere Ideen
