⭐️ Kinesiotape for Psychological Well-Being – Experiment (S21 Mini-Project)

Background

  • Taping – as prescribed by my orthopedic doctor – works very well, much better than I had expected
  • I’ve recovered my achilles tendon within two weeks of wearing tape after struggling with sharp pain for almost a month
  • The consistent presence from the tape redirected my attention to my heel several times a day
  • I believe that consistently repeated, daily attention to a part of my body helped it physically heal faster

I’ve also learned that…

  • When giving myself space and time to connect with myself, either through therapy or daily me-time, I noticed that certain psychological pains also have specific places in my body
  • For example, sadness (unsurprisingly) manifests as a sharp pain around my physical heart

What if…

  • What if you could use kinesio-tape to help support healing psychological pain as well?

    • By stimulating the physical area where it manifests
    • By consistently directing my attention to that part of my body

Prototype for sadness

  • For supporting sadness, disappointment and grief.
  • Emotions that have been very difficult for me to allow and process.

  • Hypothesis: I believe that if I tape above my physical (and emotional) heart, then I will direct my attention to my heart and its emotions more often…

    • … which I believe will result in giving emotions, including the difficult ones for me - such as sadness - more attention and Safe Space to heal and prosper

Tracking

  • Day 1: The tape is soft and nice to wear. I’ve found myself being reminded to mentally “look there” 5 times in 12 hours.
