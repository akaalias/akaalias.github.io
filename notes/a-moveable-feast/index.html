A Moveable Feast

As part of my ongoing challenge to read every day, I recently re-discovered Hemingway’s “A Moveable Feast.” 

I picked it off my shelf the other day and couldn’t put it down. It’s a perfect book to get back into a daily reading habit because of its many short, beautifully-written chapters – or short-stories. It’s deeply personal and – maybe as a cliché 40-year old white man in the thick of his midlife-crisis – the stories pull all the right strings and hit all the right notes. 

I highly recommend you give it a spin.
