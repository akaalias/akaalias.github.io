⭐️ Your amazing YOUness

Your YOUness is what makes you a one-in-a-billion event in all of time and space.

Ever.

Your YOUness is endlessly valuable to the world because you exist only once.

It’s all the books you’ve read, people you’ve met, places you have visited, places you’ve lived in, songs you heard, tools you’ve used, events that you took part in, the pain you’ve suffered, the joys you have discovered.

All of that has made you who you are and what you care about.

It has shaped how you look at the world, what you deeply value, what surprising connections you make, what incredible ideas you’ll have.

Your YOUness is is your unique point of view into the world.

It is what you can truly, honestly, effortlessly share with the world.

When I started to think about this, I was unsure what that was for me. I figured, I’d like to explore my amazing MEness.

So, with a few small exercises, I’ve started to collect what makes me uniquely, awesomely, distinctly me.
