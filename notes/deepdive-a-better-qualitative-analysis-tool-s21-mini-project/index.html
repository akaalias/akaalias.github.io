DeepDive – A better qualitative analysis tool (S21 Mini-Project)

![Screenshot 2021-04-22 at 17.57.50.png](/notes/media/Screenshot 2021-04-22 at 17.57.50.png)

Start with WHO?[^1]

I, Alexis, want a better coding tool for my qualitative analysis. …I also want to learn Swift.

Question: WHO? Answer: Me, Alexis Rondeau. Question: Who are you? Answer: I’m many things, but in this context, I’m a qualitative researcher.

  • As a…

    • Qualitative researcher in the health-industry

  • I want to…

    • Code transcripts

      • easier
      • faster

    • and Compare interviewee segments

      • clearer

  • So that…

    • I can deliver

      • shared themes
      • differences between segments
      • hot quotes
      • engaging narrative
      • clearer findings
      • deeper insights
      • more engaging narratives
      • and faster reports
      • best-informed recommendations

  • Because…

    • my clients want to

      • make better
      • more informed
      • decisions
      • look good in front of their stakeholders
      • impress their teams
      • impress their boss
      • prove their value
      • secure their employment
      • make money
      • pay for expenses
      • save money
      • invest money
      • to keep their family healthy and happy

Key Benefits

Unit/Metrics:

  • “Time to Code”
  • “Time to Surprise”
  • “Time to Insight”

MVP: “An Insights Development Environment (IDE), for you, forever.”

  • Based on Obsidian Landing Page

  • “An Insights Development Environment (IDE)”

    • Moderating interviews is such a good time to really meet someone on eye-level
    • We love connecting with people
    • Qualitative analysis is where the magic happens
    • Where you discover and develop insights from your interviews
    • Once the interviews are over and the transcripts have arrived, you hit a brick-wall.
    • It’s hard though without good tools
    • Your IDE should help you spend more quality time with your transcripts
    • In DeepDive, reading, coding, segmenting and analysing your interview transcripts is frictionless.
    • Take a first pass of coding with alias-coding,
    • review and dive deeper,
    • highlight quotes with a single keyboard stroke,
    • develop your coding book,
    • pepper in your segments and
    • marvel at what patterns emerge

  • “For you”

    • Coding interview transcripts is incredibly personal.
    • You want to connect with your interview partners after you’ve spoken with them
    • You want to also move fast and get into flow quickly
    • You also want to keep your transcripts secure
    • And you want to deliver your report ahead of time for fine-tuning with your team

    • DeepDive is…

      • Native Mac application

        • it’s much faster than any web-based application
        • It hooks into your computer directly

      • It’s built to be 100% Customizable

        • 26 Hotkeys that you can assign to your codes for speed-of-thought coding
        • 5 coding color schemes to make it easy to parse at a glance
        • Limitless coding books per project so you can fine-tune your insights
        • Limitless segmenting of your interviews so you can slice and dice during analysis
        • Built-in “Auto-Coding” that automatically tags
        • AI-Coding – Your DeepDive has a local AI engine built-in that learns as you code your transcripts and over time matures to the point of assisting you

  • “Forever”

    • “In our age when cloud services can shut down, get bought, or change privacy policy any day, the last thing you want is proprietary formats and data lock-in.”
    • With DeepDive, your data is stored locally and will not ever be held hostage by a cloud-provider
    • Export your coded transcripts anytime to fully portable Excel or CSV
    • CSV export also gives you the unparalleled interoperability to use any kind of sync, encryption, or data processing that works with plain text files.
  • Create transcripts
  • Create codings

  • Tag fragments

    • By mouse
    • By keyboard
  • See fragment stats by code

  • Auto-code

    • Add aliases to codes
    • Remove auto-codes
  • Segment transcript
  • Show strong sentiment hints

Like Delve Tool but better because…

  • No keyboard navigation
  • Webbased
  • No merging of fragments
  • No auto-coding
