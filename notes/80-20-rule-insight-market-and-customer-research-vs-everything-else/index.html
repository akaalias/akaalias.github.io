80/20 Rule Insight: Market and Customer Research vs. Everything Else

I thought about this last night and came to the following realization:

Market Research can be done fast. It’s actually pretty straightforward in a lot of cases. It may even only use 20% of the time in a venture. But it will provide more than 80% of the value and insights needed to make it work down the road.
