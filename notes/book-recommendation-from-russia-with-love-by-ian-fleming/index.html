Book Recommendation: "From Russia With Love" by Ian Fleming

HOLY SHIT. Oof. 

So, I completely forgot just how good this book was, back when I read it. I just finished “David & Goliath” and I felt like fiction and randomly picked up “From Russia With Love.” Many great things have already been said about it, so I will just give my 2 cent here. 

Imagine, this book sitting quietly in your lap. You pick it up. You draw it closer. You open it. And suddenly you’re pulled into a whirlwind of suspense, action and intrigue. Reading this book is like staring straight into the roaring engines of a Concorde. It’s loud. It’s violent. And it’s moving at 700 miles per hour. 

And you just. Can’t. Stop. Reading.

So, if you haven’t read it yet, you should. You can order it here. It’s worth every cent. Just be prepared: You won’t be able to put it down until you’re done with it.

PS: I’m reading so much because I set myself a new rule: No screens (Phone, iPad etc.) after 8pm. To improve my sleep. So, I’ve had much more time to spend reading than usual. It’s working out well so far.
