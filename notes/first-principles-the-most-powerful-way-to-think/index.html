First Principles: The Most Powerful Way To Think

Beautifully written and animated, I truly enjoyed watching this short video on what First Principles are and why they’re so powerful.

In a way, I want to believe that I, too, am a seeker of understanding the world deeply in order to be able to:

  • Innovate
  • Optimize
  • Learn
  • Teach

I highly recommend you watch this video below right away.

Don’t think you have time to watch right now? Imagine how much time you can save in the future by improving your thinking right now? Plus, it’s less than 10 minutes and I found it very entertaining.

Sounds too complicated? The narration and animations are so simple and so clear that I can promise you, you will understand it.

Sounds too philosophical? The essay is surprisingly concrete and uses a lot of tangible examples to make the point.

So, without further ado: Enjoy!
