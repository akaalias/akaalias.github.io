NoteFriday, June 5, 2020 · 00:00

05.06.2020

Noch ein weiterer Eintrag für heute…

I did 2 hours of Deep Work – got the funnel ready and even found a good name for the overarching brand: MindSky Academy. Nice! Got the domain, that’s verifying, but once it is, I’ll finalize the Google Suite thing and then head over to mailerlite to verify my account with my fancy alexis@mindskyacademy.com email address. Pretty cool!

I’m happy it’s the end of the week, I’m not that sharp anymore after working every day since Monday but I feel like I’ve pushed myself forward and kept good records of every amount of effort I put in. In total it’s 14 hours of Deep Work (not counting work for C Space) working on my MindSky funnel and products and copy and videos. 14 hours I’ve invested in myself and my future and that worked. I need to eat more throughout the day, that was the only issue I haven’t tackled yet. I’ll try HUEL to make sure I eat something until I have the time to cook a meal.

So yeah, I’m pretty stoked about this week! I bought myself flowers that I put on my desk and that makes it nice to sit here. They have a calming effect and are beautiful to look at. I think they’re enjoying their stay. I hope.

Since I cleaned up my desk last week and continued to remove anything that wasn’t essential from it I like working here much more. It’s the bare necessities I need to work and no distractions. The checklist above the table has also helped tremendously. I don’t yet know how to ensure 100% that I start a timer before I go into work but it’s working. I’m missing 20% maximum of sessions from my timer this week. That happens when I’m just in the zone I think. The little triangle stars I’m using to reward and celebrate are cute. I wish there were more of them. Or different stickers. And those that I don’t know what it’s going to be ahead of time (like a variable reward kind of thing) – I’ll look into that next week. Maybe Modulor is open again or that toy store down the street.

In either case, I feel like I’m developing, rapidly, a way for me to work hard and long and feel good about it. This is a major progress for me from staring into space or sleeping to working and getting things done consistently. I am proud that I’ve found a solution to my problem (Ritalin) and am excited to be ABLE to shape my life the way I want it to.

YES!
