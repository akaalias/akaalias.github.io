Things We Also Like: \"Whole Foods\" vs. \"Trader Joes\" in the US

I’m a big fan of Trader Joes. My friend Chris loves Whole Foods.

So I had an idea.

I figured I could generate a visual comparison report. A report that would let me see how the “Trader Joe” people and the “Whole Foods” people relate and how they differ. Just out of curiosity.

My goal is to become a ‘student of markets. And Facebook Audience Insights already provides ample data but it’s not very visual. So I decided to build a small tool that turns a list of related likes for a specific audience into a mosaic of the logos of the pages liked.

Just a few things I noticed off the bat:

  • “Trader Joes” also like Whole Foods but not the other way around.
  • “Trader Joes” seem to like more public figures, like Rachel Maddow and Dan Rather
  • PBS, NPR, Etsy, REI and Burt’s Bees and Pottery Barn are equally liked by both groups
