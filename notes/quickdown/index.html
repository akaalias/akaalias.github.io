QuickDown

Demo

Who

  • For myself:
  • As an avid Obsidian user who…
  • Often doesn’t have Obsidian running because I prefer to have only the app I’m working in running at a time
  • Loves taking many small markdown notes for my vault
  • Uses MacOS

What

  • A minimal markdown note-taking menu bar app for MacOS
  • Quickly and lets me seamlessly open, write and save markdown notes into my Obsidian vault
  • Designed to be completely keyboard-first

Why

  • I want to quickly save small notes into my Obsidian vault without having Obsidian running or having to open it
  • As a keyboard-first person, I want to capture an idea while 100% staying on my keyboard
  • None of the existing menu-bar apps provide the minimal configuration I am looking for: Focus on keyboard-first and simple, no-frills markdown support

How

  • Keyboard-first note-taking
  • Seamless writing with a single global hotkey that opens and focuses the note-field (Command-Option-N)
  • Direct saving to Obsidian vault with standard hotkey (Command-S)

Repository

  • https://github.com/akaalias/quickdown (Private – Let me know if you want it on the App Store!)
