This is INSANE: In just 4 days, our campaign generated 1.283 qualified leads for just 200€. That's a 0,16€ cost per lead.

I’m really proud of the results that a campaign I’ve created for a consulting client of mine has produced. I’ve been running an initial advertising/marketing experiment for them, and by choosing the right audience and designing the right offer, the campaign generated highly qualified leads at 0,16€ each. 0,16€!

At a high level, that means: We spent 200€ and generated 1.286 Leads at a conversion-rate of almost 4%.

In other words, I can help you – for as little as 16 cent each – get you your ideal customers - over and over - through your door, into your store and in front of your products or services.
