Meine größten Stärken – My Strongest Talents

  1. Restorative: People exceptionally talented in the Restorative theme are adept at dealing with problems. They are good at figuring out what is wrong and resolving it.
  2. Ideation: People exceptionally talented in the Ideation theme are fascinated by ideas. They are able to find connections between seemingly disparate phenomena.
  3. Relator: People exceptionally talented in the Relator theme enjoy close relationships with others. They find deep satisfaction in working hard with friends to achieve a goal.
  4. Input: People exceptionally talented in the Input theme have a need to collect and archive. They may accumulate information, ideas, artifacts or even relationships.
  5. Empathy: People exceptionally talented in the Empathy theme can sense other people’s feelings by imagining themselves in others’ lives or situations.
  6. Restorative – Executing
  7. Ideation – Strategic Thinking
  8. Relator – Relationship Building
  9. Input – Strategic Thinking
  10. Empathy – Relationship Building
  11. Adaptability – Relationship Building
  12. Positivity – Relationship Building
  13. Strategic – Strategic Thinking
  14. Learner – Strategic Thinking

I Lead With Relationship Building Themes

Those who lead through Relationship Building are the essential glue that holds a team together.

Without these strengths on a team, in many cases, the group is simply a composite of individuals.

In contrast, leaders with exceptional Relationship Building strength have the unique ability to create groups and organizations that are much greater than the sum of their parts.

THE POWER OF MY TALENTS

My unique talents for Restorative, Ideation, Relator, Input and Empathy are the most powerful starting point for living my strongest life possible.

DEVELOPING MY FIVE STRENGTHS TO MAXIMIZE MY POTENTIAL

My greatest chance to succeed — at work or anywhere else — lies in strengthening what I naturally do best and doing more of it.

QuickDown

Demo

Who

  • For myself:
  • As an avid Obsidian user who…
  • Often doesn’t have Obsidian running because I prefer to have only the app I’m working in running at a time
  • Loves taking many small markdown notes for my vault
  • Uses MacOS

What

  • A minimal markdown note-taking menu bar app for MacOS
  • Quickly and lets me seamlessly open, write and save markdown notes into my Obsidian vault
  • Designed to be completely keyboard-first

Why

  • I want to quickly save small notes into my Obsidian vault without having Obsidian running or having to open it
  • As a keyboard-first person, I want to capture an idea while 100% staying on my keyboard
  • None of the existing menu-bar apps provide the minimal configuration I am looking for: Focus on keyboard-first and simple, no-frills markdown support

How

  • Keyboard-first note-taking
  • Seamless writing with a single global hotkey that opens and focuses the note-field (Command-Option-N)
  • Direct saving to Obsidian vault with standard hotkey (Command-S)

Repository

  • https://github.com/akaalias/quickdown (Private – Let me know if you want it on the App Store!)

Settings

  • Gear-button bottom-right to access ![Screenshot 2021-12-08 at 16.37.00.png](/notes/media/Screenshot 2021-12-08 at 16.37.00.png)
  • Modal overlay with basic hotkeys ![Screenshot 2021-12-08 at 16.36.20.png](/notes/media/Screenshot 2021-12-08 at 16.36.20.png)
