Currently Re-Reading: Malcom Gladwell's "David & Goliath"

It’s funny how, sometimes you can simply ‘ask’ your subconscious to pick the right book for you by just ‘mindlessly’ browsing your bookshelf. 

That exactly happened yesterday to me. I had just finished “A Moveable Feast” the day before and, since my new rule is to have no screen on after 8pm, I was ready to read something new. 

Just looking around, I quickly and without thinking picked out “David & Goliath” and within minutes of reading, I was hooked – again. I remember how much I enjoyed reading it a few years back (Maybe even while still in New York?) And, as magic will have it, It’s one of those stories that I can relate to very much at the moment.

So, I highly recommend it.
