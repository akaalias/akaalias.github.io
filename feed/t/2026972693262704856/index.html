Looking back, I am so grateful and lucky that my godmother Diane had subscribed to @NatGeo throughout the 80s. You could pick any issue from the stack and travel the world instantly.

It's crazy: I can still open any issue from 1985 and recognize every cover, most of the photos and ads and even some of the stories.

Of course, Jame Gurney's Rockwell-y soybean tableau was, and still is, an all-time favorite!

So, on behalf that 8 year-old kid growing up in West Germany and this one adult who has lived in many countries since:

Thank you Diane and thank you National Geographic.
