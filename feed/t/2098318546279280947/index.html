If you're waking up this morning grateful for another day...

You've already won, friend.

My life hack for this: a little golf counter, the clicker kind. I got a fancy one in silver and I carry it with me. When people say "count your blessings" I took that literally.

A few times a day I stop and ask what I could be thankful for, then click. Nice coffee, click, thank you. Easily 20-30 a day, from the mundane to the very high-spirited.

Today so far: my lovely mechanical keyboard, the guy who just delivered groceries, green tea.

What are you grateful for today?
