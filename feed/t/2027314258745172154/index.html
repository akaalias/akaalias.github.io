I briefly believed that "cron job" or "claws" would be the words of the year 2026.

Now I believe it'll be "AINXIETY".

...

"Don't believe the hype!" said the great prophet Carlton Douglas Ridenhour and his friend, the wise Bodhisattva William Jonathan Drayton Jr., in unisono, in 1988.

And they were right. Do not believe the hype. If this AI doomerism is getting to you: Take a break. Take care of yourself. Take a nice long shower. Eat something yummy. Call a friend!

And yes, also "do look up." There is so much borderline magic stuff happening right now. You ARE sitting front-row at Dylan's 1965 performance at the Newport Folk Festival.

Things change. They always have and will. The good news is: We humans are actually really good at change. YOU are really good at this.

With love,

Alexis
