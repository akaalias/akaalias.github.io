Another @openclaw -related hot take: Most AI-generated code will mostly be used by other AIs, not humans.

For context: Out of fear of downloading compromised skills, I've disabled my assistant from getting skills from clawdhub altogether.

Instead I let it write custom software directly for itself. On demand. And from scratch.

So, if I need a skill to connect to Todoist, I just let it write that for itself. Takes 20 seconds and is good enough to move ahead.
