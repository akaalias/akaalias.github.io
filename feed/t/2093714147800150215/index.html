One reason I still read books:

They slow me down.

Most of the internet rewards speed. Scan it. React to it. Move on to the next thing.

A good book does the opposite.

It asks you to sit with someone else’s thinking for a few hours. Sometimes you agree. Sometimes you argue with the author in your head. Sometimes one paragraph stays with you for days.

I like that.

Not everything valuable should be consumed quickly.
