@AmazonKindle I love, love, love every single Kindle I've ever bought.

And speed absolutely matters:

My latest one, an early 2025 Kindle Paperwhite, is so fast that I wound up reading Pratchett's 42-volume Discworld series in less than 12 months.

And yet, since buying my first Kindle, I still have one single feature request:

After I've continued reading a book on my Kindle
And I've then locked the screen
When I look at the lock screen
I want to see the current page that I had been reading
So that whenever I glance at my locked Kindle
I can quickly read a sentence or two of what I was reading
And pull myself back into continue reading that book
And keep my reading momentum
And finish more books sooner.

The "whenever I glance at my Kindle" happens relatively often and neither the book cover nor the default graphics help me keep momentum the way the "open last page" would.

Thank you for your consideration!
