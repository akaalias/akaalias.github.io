If sperm whales could talk, what would they say?

New research on their communication reveals a complex combinatorial system that challenges our understanding of animal vocalizations.

By analyzing 8,719 whale click sounds called “codas”, using machine learning, MIT CSAIL & Project CETI scientists uncovered a nuanced "sperm whale phonetic alphabet.”
