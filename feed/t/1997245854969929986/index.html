Idea for "marking" AI-generated text: Make a final pass that replaces the regular letters with UTF8 homoglyphs instead. Still 100% readable and verifiably of non-human origin.

Case in point, here's the homoglyph version of this message:

ꓲdеа fоr "mаrkіոց" ꓮꓲ-ցеոеrаtеd tехt: ꓟаkе а fіոаꓲ раѕѕ tһаt rерꓲасеѕ tһе rеցսꓲаr ꓲеttеrѕ ԝіtһ ꓴꓔꓝ8 һоmоցꓲурһѕ іոѕtеаd. ꓢtіꓲꓲ 100% rеаdаbꓲе аոd νеrіfіаbꓲу оf ոоո-һսmаո оrіցіո.

This conversation doesn't need to be covert either. In fact, I think that AI-generated text should have it's own visual voice! And it looks like, for the Latin alphabet at least, there are several 'typefaces" that could be built.
