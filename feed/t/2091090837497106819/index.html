I fixed my YouTube feed by (rigorously) using the "Do not recommend videos from this channel" for a month.

Just a heads-up: It felt like strangling a beast. For the first rounds your feed cleans up quickly but then their algorithm fights back with even shittier, top-of-bell-curve slop that you had initially.

You gotta keep blocking channels until suddenly the feed stays clean and tight.

Good luck! You'll need it but it's worth it.
