Infermation (/ɪnfərˈmeɪʃən/; portmanteau of inference and information) is knowledge produced by inferential processes rather than direct observation, measurement, or first-hand transmission.

Unlike conventional information, which is grounded in a source, infermation is derived — synthesized from patterns, priors, and partial evidence.
