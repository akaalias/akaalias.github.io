cc'ing @emollick and @ryancarson: 20 years later, "Air" by @geoffryman feels surprisingly more urgent and nuanced in explaining the before and after of the global AI rollout.

> Air is the story of a town's fashion expert Chung Mae, a smart but illiterate peasant woman in a small village in the fictional country of Karzistan (loosely based on the country of Kazakhstan), and her suddenly leading role in reaction to dramatic, worldwide experiments with a new information technology called Air.
> Air is information exchange, not unlike the Internet, that occurs in everyone's brain and is intended to connect the world. After a test of Air is imposed on Mae's unprepared mountain town, everyone and everything changes, especially Mae who was deeper into Air than any other person.
> Afterwards, Mae struggles to prepare her people for what is to come while learning all about the world outside her home.
