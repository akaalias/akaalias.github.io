How to Create Outlines for Publishing Faster - Journey for Obsidian Plugin Demo

NEWS:

  • 🎉 The plugin is officially released in the Obsidian Plugin Directory 🎉

Hey there! Alexis here - Obsidian enthusiast and student at the University of Life! I want to show you a new Obsidian plugin I've been working on. This plugin automatically creates an outline for your publishing (An outline for an article, a blog post, or even a book!).

Why? I’ve noticed that my creative output using Obsidian hasn't been as prolific as I’d like it to be. I have hundreds of atomic notes, I’ve followed every best-practice, but right now, Obsidian doesn’t help me take that next step.

It’s really hard to extract a good story from my vault.

That's why I've created this new plugin :)
